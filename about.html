M2MOKO is a contemporary clothing brand and store based in Stone Town, Zanzibar. It makes relaxed, expressive pieces and ships them around the world.

The brand

Clothing with a strong sense of place

The M2MOKO look is calm and generous — coordinated sets, softly tailored shirts, wide trousers and tunic tops, in colours drawn from sand, sea and island light.

The brand describes its clothing as “Made in Zanzibar,” with worldwide shipping. You can shop online or visit the store on Soko Muhogo Street in Stone Town.
