Client Care

Shipping, returns and exchanges, privacy and terms. The sections below are drafting placeholders — each needs confirmed detail from M2MOKO before the store can launch or take orders.

Shipping  •  Returns & exchanges  •  Privacy  •  Terms

Shipping

M2MOKO states that it ships worldwide, and offers local delivery and in-store pickup in Zanzibar. To publish a real shipping policy the brand needs to confirm:

  • Countries and regions shipped to, and any exclusions
  • Couriers used for local, regional and international delivery
  • Rates, free-shipping thresholds, and the currency charged
  • Handling time for in-stock pieces vs. made-to-order production time
  • Estimated delivery times per zone, and tracking
  • Duties and taxes — who pays, and how they are shown at checkout
  • Packaging, and the process for lost or damaged parcels
  • Local delivery area and pickup instructions for the Stone Town store

Returns & exchanges

No returns policy is published yet. Online checkout must not open until this is confirmed. M2MOKO needs to define:

  • The return and exchange window, and how it is measured
  • Condition requirements (unworn, tags attached, original packaging)
  • Whether made-to-order or custom pieces are excluded
  • Who pays return shipping, domestically and internationally
  • Refund method and timing, and whether exchanges are offered
  • How faulty or incorrect items are handled
  • Any rules specific to international returns and customs

Privacy

A privacy policy will be finalised alongside the platform build. It should cover the data M2MOKO collects (order, contact, payment and browsing data), how it is used and stored, third parties involved (the store platform, payment provider, couriers, email and analytics tools), cookie and consent handling, data-retention periods, and how a customer can request access or deletion. A named contact and business address for privacy requests is required.

Terms of service

Terms of service will be finalised with M2MOKO and, where needed, a legal adviser. They should cover order acceptance and cancellation, pricing and currency, payment terms, made-to-order and custom-order conditions (deposits, lead times, revisions, cancellation), intellectual property, acceptable use, liability limits, and the governing law and jurisdiction — based on M2MOKO’s country of business registration.
