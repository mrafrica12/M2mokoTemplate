The brand

A studio and store in Stone Town

M2MOKO makes relaxed, contemporary clothing in Zanzibar and ships it around the world. The brand describes its work simply: “Made in Zanzibar.”

The look is unhurried — generous cuts, warm natural colours and an ease that suits the island and travels well beyond it. You can shop online, or find M2MOKO on Soko Muhogo Street in Stone Town.
